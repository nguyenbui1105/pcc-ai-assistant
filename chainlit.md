# PCC AI Assistant

Welcome! I'm your AI assistant for Portland Community College.

**What I can help with:**

- **Your courses & schedule** — "What classes am I taking this term?"
- **Degree progress** — "How many credits do I have left for AAOT?"
- **Financial aid** — "What's my current balance?"
- **Course recommendations** — "What courses should I take next?"
- **Build a schedule** — "Make me a schedule for next term with no Friday classes"
- **PCC policies** — "What is the attendance policy?" / "How do I apply for a leave of absence?"

---

*Running in demo mode (sample student "Alex"). To use your real PCC data, run `python scripts/setup_session.py` to log in and save your session.*
